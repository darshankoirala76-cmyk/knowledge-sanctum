About The Knowledge Sanctum

The Knowledge Sanctum is a digital universe dedicated to knowledge, discovery, and exploration. From science and technology to history, philosophy, Sanskrit, and the mysteries of our universe, it brings ideas together through informative articles, documentaries, and AI-powered learning experiences. Created by Darshan Koirala, a student of Khangchendzonga State University, Sikkim, its mission is to inspire curiosity, encourage critical thinking, and make learning an engaging journey for everyone.
